Kai's Calm Quest

A puzzle game where kids clear a storm by actually breathing.

Takes about 2 minutes. Best on a phone with the sound on.

How to play

The first level walks you through all of this with a guiding hand.

1Match 3

Slide an orb onto the one next to it so three of the same element line up. They pop.

2Fill Luma

Every match fills Luma, the fox in the bottom corner. When her ring is full she glows. Tap her.

↑ in
↓ out

3Breathe with her

Breathe in: press Luma and slide her up slowly with the glowing ring. Breathe out: slide her back down, or turn on the mic and blow gently on the dandelion. Going too fast doesn't work.

4Clear the storm

A slow, deep breath blows away 4 clouds. A rushed one blows away 1. Clear every cloud to win the level.

On a laptop? Use the mouse to drag orbs and Luma. When the game asks how to breathe out, pick Trace it with a finger.

Why this matters

  • Kids are told to "calm down" and never shown how. Here the breath is the only way to win, so every level is practice.
  • The game scores the breath itself. Slow, long breaths win, so kids learn the pace that actually settles the body.
  • It's built on Demaris's qigong practice: the five elements, each with its own colour, symbol, organ, emotion, and sound, straight off his teaching sheets.
  • Each level takes one hard feeling and moves it: worry to trust, anger to kindness, too much to peace, sadness to strength, fear to wisdom.
  • Every level ends with a real practice kids can do off the screen, with a guided breath from Sorin.

The five elements

Every orb is an element off Demaris's teaching sheets. Each carries a feeling, and the level teaches the shift.

The cycle is the bonus. Wood feeds Fire, Fire makes Earth, Earth holds Metal, Metal carries Water, Water grows Wood. Match along that circle and Luma fills faster, so kids learn the creation cycle by playing it.

How it makes money

  • The game is free, always. No price to download and no paywall on levels.
  • No forced ads. Nothing interrupts a level. Parents can trust it and kids just play.
  • We sell extras: power-up bundles, heart refills, and time-limited boosts like double coins for 30 minutes. Demo prices ($0.99 to $1.99) are placeholders, and nothing is charged.
  • Optional reward videos: a player can choose to watch a short video for a free heart, power-up, or boost.
  • Good breaths earn coins too, so kids who practice can keep playing without paying.
