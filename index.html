Built on real practice

Demaris's five elements, as rules of play

Wood feeds Fire, Fire makes Earth, Earth holds Metal, Metal carries Water, Water grows Wood. A match lights up the next element. Obstacles break by the element that controls them. Each place pairs an element with an organ, a feeling, a sound, a breath and a qigong animal from Demaris's teaching. Storm clouds melt when you match beside them, and a slow breath blows them away.

    For grown-upsWhy MorQi
    MorQi
    Calm is a skill. MorQi makes it a game.

    Your child wins every level by slowing their breath, then meets the feeling behind the storm. Little by little, a slow out-breath becomes the thing they reach for when a big feeling shows up.

    This week

    Practice time

    Pick a moment for Sorin's practice. At that time the Practice button glows so it becomes part of the day.

    What your child practises

    BreatheEvery level is cleared with a real, slow breath: in through the nose, a longer breath out. A long out-breath is one of the simplest ways to help the body settle.
    Name itEach episode is about one feeling, told at your child's age. Naming a feeling makes it smaller and easier to talk about.
    Turn itFrom qigong's five elements: worry can turn into trust, anger into kindness, fear into wisdom. Feelings are never bad. They are energy that can change.

    Grows with your child

    Our promises

    • Free to play, always. No forced ads, no timers that pressure your child.
    • No chat, no leaderboards, no strangers. Your child only plays with Kai's village.
    • Small extras only, behind a grown-up gate. $0.99, $1.99 or $4.99, never pushed.
    • Feelings are never "bad". School-safe language throughout.

    • Baby mode for the littlest ones: five gentle boards play themselves with no words on screen, Luma breathes right over the board, and every touch, swipe and helper makes something happen.
    Try it together tonight Breathe in for 4, out for 6. Three times. Ask what felt different.
