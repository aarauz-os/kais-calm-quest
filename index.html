Built on real practice

Demaris's five elements, as rules of play

Wood feeds Fire, Fire makes Earth, Earth holds Metal, Metal carries Water, Water grows Wood. A match lights up the next element. Obstacles break by the element that controls them. Each place pairs an element with an organ, a feeling, a sound and a breath from Demaris's qigong teaching sheets.

Fair by design
  • Free forever. No paywall, no forced ads.
  • Extras only. The headline offer: Nine Days of Calm, $1.99 for nine days.
  • A grown-up approves every purchase. No countdowns, no pressure.
  • School-safe. Feelings are never "bad". No leaderboards, no shame.
